## Program Brief

Harborlight Community Learning ran the fictional Neighborhood Learning Lab from
January 12, 2026 through March 6, 2026. The program served elementary students
through after-school reading practice, vocabulary games, paired read-aloud
sessions, and facilitator-supported reflection.

The Community Learning Catalyst Fund requested a short progress update covering
activities delivered, participation, measured reading progress, participant
experience, and improvement plans.

## Attendance Log Summary

- 22 students enrolled.
- 20 students attended at least once.
- 16 sessions were scheduled.
- 15 sessions were delivered.
- 1 session was cancelled because of a fictional weather closure.
- Delivered sessions recorded 246 total participant-session attendances.
- Average attendance was 16.4 participants per delivered session.

## Activity Notes

Facilitators used vocabulary sorting, paired read-aloud practice, short
comprehension checks, and reading games during the delivered sessions.

The team noted that the paired read-aloud format helped quieter participants
practice before sharing with the whole group.

## Reading Check Summary

18 participants had matched pre/post reading-comprehension checks.

The matched group had a mean pre score of 61 percent and a mean post score of
74 percent. That is a 13 percentage-point increase against a 10-point target.

14 of the 18 matched participants improved by at least 5 points. 3 were
unchanged within tolerance. 1 declined.

This is descriptive progress only. The packet does not include a comparison
group, school-grade records, or enough evidence to claim causal impact.

## Participant Experience
