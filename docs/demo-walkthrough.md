# Demo Walkthrough

The first demo uses the fictional Neighborhood Learning Lab packet.

1. Load the synthetic Winter Learning Lab sample.
2. Review the funder brief and four requirements.
3. Confirm candidate evidence in the ledger.
4. Inspect the attendance and reading-comprehension metrics.
5. Generate or load the evidence-linked draft.
6. Click a quantitative claim and inspect its period, denominator, and source.
7. Show the unsupported confidence/grades claim blocked or downgraded.
8. Export Markdown or print view with warnings and evidence appendix visible.

The trust moment is the refusal to present unsupported confidence or future
grade improvement as measured impact.
